Win three in a row on day one and you're still in Bronze. Your rank is your rating minus three times our doubt, and on day one we doubt a lot.

Your first five ranked games are placement. You tell us beginner, intermediate or advanced. The games decide.

New season, no wipe. We just get a little less sure of everyone, and good players climb back fast.

Casual games keep their own hidden rating. It only exists to set up fair friendlies.
